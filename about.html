KEY FEATURES

Everything a farmer needs to make a better decision.

01

Weather & Soil Advisory

Regional weather and soil insights help farmers understand suitable conditions and what action to take.

02

Smart Crop Explorer

Explore suitable crops by category with cultivation duration, sowing period, fertilizer needs and market opportunities.

03

Mandi & Seed Intelligence

Compare mandi selling rates, market trends, potential price peaks and seed buying prices.

04

Profit Simulator

Estimate revenue, cultivation costs, transport expenses and net profit before making a crop decision.

05

Cultivation Roadmap

Follow a structured crop lifecycle from seed preparation and sowing to crop care, harvest and mandi dispatch.

06

Shared Transport

Connect farmers travelling toward the same mandi to make small-volume transportation more practical.

07

Mandi Price Alerts

Set price triggers and receive an alert when important mandi price conditions are reached.

Multilingual & Location-Aware
